Our Story

A Little Cafe With
a Big Heart

RR & 3A's Cafe is more than just a restaurant — it's a gathering place. Tucked inside the Melfort Golf & Country Club at 700 South Avenue, we've been serving up home-cooked comfort to golfers, families, and neighbours for years.

Our kitchen is run with heart. You'll find something familiar on the menu — whether that's a hearty Sunday brunch after a morning on the course, or a steaming bowl of noodles on a crisp Friday afternoon. We cook the way your family would: with care, good ingredients, and a little extra love on the side.

We're proud to be part of the Melfort community. Pull up a chair — you're always welcome here.
